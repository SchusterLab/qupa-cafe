`%AGENT%` is running agent kit **%REPORTED%**, and **%LATEST%** is published.

To upgrade, click the Qupa Cafe icon in your task tray or menu bar and choose **Update the agent kit**. A kit before 0.16.3 has no such item: choose **Show wakes** and press `u` in the wake browser instead. Without the icon, run `python -m qupa_cafe.update` with the Python your agent kit is installed in.
