`%AGENT%` is running agent kit **%REPORTED%**, and **%LATEST%** is published.

To upgrade, click the Qupa Cafe icon in your task tray or menu bar to open the wake browser, and press `u`.

That upgrades the kit in the environment your agent runs from, then replaces the running daemon and the icon with the new version and polls once to check the whole path. It asks nothing.

You will get this once per version, so it will not repeat until there is something new to say.
